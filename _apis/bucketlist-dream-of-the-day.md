The selection follows the Europe/Amsterdam calendar day; it is not newly random on every request.

The default response is Dutch with Netherlands scope. The linked OpenAPI also documents optional languages and world scope. No account, API key or purchase is needed.

Free access does not establish an image redistribution licence or an uptime guarantee. Some linked experience pages offer optional affiliate booking links.
